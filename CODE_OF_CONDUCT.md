# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone — regardless of experience level,
background, or identity. Detection engineering is a craft people learn; questions and first
contributions are encouraged.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique the detection, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Explain *why*, share sources, and help others learn.
- Keep it professional: no harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, trolling, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may
ban repeat or severe offenders. Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer
privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
